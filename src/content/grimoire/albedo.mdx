# Albedo

**Albedo**, or the "whitening," is the second stage of the alchemical *Magnum Opus*. After the darkness of Nigredo, the Albedo represents purification and the return of light.

This is the stage of **illumination and clarity**. The ashes of the ego have been cleared, and the soul begins to see itself with honesty and compassion. In this stage, the alchemist washes away the impurities, bringing the spirit into a state of equilibrium.

## The Alchemical Process
- **Symbolism**: The White Swan, the Moon, the silver mirror.
- **Experience**: Peace, insight, emotional healing.
- **Outcome**: A purified consciousness, ready for the final fusion.

The Albedo is the breath of fresh air after the storm of the shadow, preparing the vessel for the fiery intensity of **Rubedo**.
