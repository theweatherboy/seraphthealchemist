# The Emerald Tablet

The **Emerald Tablet** is one of the most pivotal texts in the history of alchemy and hermeticism. It is attributed to Hermes Trismegistus and contains the core principle of the universe:

> *"As above, so below, as below, so above."*

This principle suggests that the microcosm (the individual) reflects the macrocosm (the universe), and that by understanding one, we can understand the other.

## Key Concepts
- **Correspondence**: The link between different levels of existence.
- **Transmutation**: The process of changing one substance or state of being into another.
- **Unity**: The belief that all things originate from a single source.

In the *Seraph, The Alchemist* grimoire, the Emerald Tablet serves as the foundation for our study of **Spiritual Alchemy**.
