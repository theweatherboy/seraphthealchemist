# Nigredo

**Nigredo**, or the "blackening," is the first stage of the alchemical *Magnum Opus*. It represents the process of putrefaction, decomposition, and the descent into the darkness.

In spiritual alchemy, Nigredo is the stage of **Shadow Work**. It is the necessary breakdown of the ego, where we confront our fears, traumas, and the hidden parts of ourselves. Without this dissolution, transformation is impossible.

## The Alchemical Process
- **Symbolism**: The Raven, the skull, the void.
- **Experience**: Melancholy, confusion, deep introspection.
- **Outcome**: The purification of the soul by stripping away the false self.

Nigredo is the most challenging phase, as it requires us to sit in the darkness before the first light of **Albedo** can emerge.
