# The Great Cycle

The **Great Cycle** is the cosmic rhythm of expansion and contraction, creation and destruction. It is the spiritual equivalent of the breath: the universe exhales (Creation/Expansion) and then inhales (Destruction/Contraction).

We currently reside in the transition between cycles. This is why the current era is one of such intense transformation and volatility—the old Pattern is dissolving to make way for the new.

## The Stages of the Cycle
- **The Dawn**: The emergence of consciousness from the void.
- **The Zenith**: The peak of complexity, knowledge, and material manifestation.
- **The Dusk**: The gradual return to simplicity and the shedding of illusions.
- **The Void**: The moment of absolute silence before the next exhale.

Those who understand the Great Cycle do not fear death or ending, for they know that every "end" is simply the beginning of the next inhalation.
